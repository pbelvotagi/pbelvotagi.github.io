About me

I am a sixth year graduate student in mathematics at UC San Diego. My advisor is Kiran Kedlaya. I completed my BS-MS at IISER Pune. My research interest is in p-adic differential equations and Berkovich geometry.

I served as the President of the Association for Women in Mathematics (AWM) at UC San Diego from 2021 to 2023. We organized community building, diversity and outreach activities. Before that I served as the mentorship coordinator.
